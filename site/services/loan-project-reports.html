What we need from you

  • Last 2–3 years' financial statements or books (for expansion loans)
  • Cost estimate of the project — machinery, working capital, etc.
  • Loan amount and repayment period you're seeking
  • Business registration documents (Udyam, GST, PAN)

Why a CA-prepared report matters

  • Bank credit teams scrutinise ratios and assumptions closely — a weak report causes delays or rejection
  • Projections need to be realistic and internally consistent, not just optimistic
  • A signed CMA report carries more weight with the sanctioning authority
